# Brief review

Recommendations on the brief, made before any design work. Each one is backed by what the competitor review found.

## What the brief gets right

- The two audiences, companies and candidates, are clearly identified, and the home page is already designed around them.
- Role coverage is mapped clearly across four practices: Technology, Product, Data & AI, Leadership.
- Insights, clients and testimonials are planned for a later phase, with space reserved now.

## Seven recommendations

### 1. Shorten the candidate form

The brief lists 20 fields in one pass, with the heaviest ones early: full CTC breakup (fixed, variable, bonus, ESOPs, other, total), reason for a resume gap, reason for a change, hometown.

ABC Consultants, the largest brand on the competitor list, asks 16 visible fields and takes Current CTC as **one number**.

**Recommendation:** split the form into two steps. The public step has nine fields. Everything else is optional, on the confirmation screen. Full detail is in the Forms section.

### 2. Move "reason for gap in resume" to the recruiter call

Asked at first contact, it reads as screening before a conversation. That works against a firm whose pitch is attracting top talent.

### 3. Confirm whether the 5,000 candidate pool exists today

The brief uses the number two ways. In the vision it is a goal ("to attract ... with a ready pool of 5,000+"). On the Why Us page it is stated as a current asset. If the pool does not exist at launch, publishing it is a credibility risk.

The parent site, trcconsulting.org, already publishes "5,000+ Projects". Using the same number for candidates on a sister site will look recycled unless it is a separately counted figure.

### 4. Cover data protection properly

The site collects resumes, salary details, education and contact information, then shares them with hiring companies. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies. The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025, and summaries from PIB and DSCI give 13 May 2027 as the date the main obligations apply.

The brief marks consent as a *suggested* field with wording to be confirmed, and the page list has no privacy policy.

**Recommendation:** consent is mandatory, the notice states what is collected and who it is shared with, there is a retention and deletion position, and a privacy policy page exists. TRC's legal team drafts and approves this wording.

### 5. Do not rely on email alone for submissions

Resumes arriving as attachments in a shared inbox will not hold past the first weeks, and it is weak on data protection.

**Recommendation:** every submission writes to a database, resumes go to private file storage, and an email notification goes to the tech hiring inbox. In Next.js one Server Action can do all three. TRC to confirm which ATS or CRM it uses, if any.

### 6. No jobs board in phase one

8 of the 11 competitors do not run one. The two real boards belong to HuntingCube, which is a product company, and Careernet, which moved its board onto a separate domain. ABC Consultants takes candidate registrations with no roles listed, which is exactly TRC's model.

### 7. Give each practice its own page

The brief has one "What we do" page. Success Pact runs 13 separate service pages and Zyoin runs 10. Purple Quarter goes further, with one page per job title (CTO, CPO, CISO, VP Engineering and more). One combined page cannot compete with four dedicated ones in search.

**Recommendation:** four pillar pages in phase one, role pages in phase two. Detail is in the Sitemap section.

## Missing from the brief

- Domain: a new domain, or a section of trcconsulting.org
- CMS, and who updates content after launch
- Who writes the copy
- Timeline and budget
- Geography: India only, or also the UAE where the parent has offices
- Analytics, SEO and accessibility requirements
