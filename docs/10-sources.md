## Usability

- [NN/g, "Scrolling and Attention", Therese Fessenden, 15 April 2018](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/): 57% of viewing time above the fold, 74% in the first two screens; 120 participants
- [NN/g, "Auto-Forwarding Carousels and Accordions Annoy Users and Reduce Visibility", Jakob Nielsen, 19 January 2013](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/auto-forwarding/): the case against auto-forwarding carousels. The hero has one at the agency's request, so it pauses on hover, focus and off screen, stops once someone picks a card, and has a pause button
- [NN/g, "Homepage Design: 5 Fundamental Principles", Huei-Hsin Wang, 15 March 2024](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/): easy access home, say who you are, show examples, prompt action, keep it simple and avoid popups
- [NN/g, "Hamburger Menus and Hidden Navigation Hurt UX Metrics", Kara Pernice and Raluca Budiu, 26 June 2016](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/hamburger-menus/): hidden navigation cut discoverability by more than 20%
- [NN/g, "Website Forms Usability: Top 10 Recommendations", Kathryn Whitenton, 1 May 2016](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/): short, single column, labels above, specific errors

## Motion

Checked on 26 October 2026.

- [GSAP licence](https://gsap.com/standard-license): GSAP is free for everyone, with Webflow's support; the full terms are linked from that page
- [GSAP, React guide](https://gsap.com/resources/React/): `useGSAP`, scoping, automatic cleanup through `gsap.context`, client components in Next.js
- [GSAP, gsap.matchMedia()](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia()): conditions for breakpoints and `prefers-reduced-motion`, automatic revert
- [Motion, Reduce bundle size](https://motion.dev/docs/react-reduce-bundle-size): `LazyMotion` and `m` (about 5KB against 43KB for `motion`), `domAnimation` (+24KB), `domMax` (+38KB), lazy loading the features
- [NN/g, "Executing UX Animations: Duration and Motion Characteristics", Page Laubheimer, 9 February 2020](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/): most UI animation between 100 and 400ms, ease-out on entry, shorter exits, respect reduced motion
- [GSAP, "Most common GSAP mistakes"](https://gsap.com/resources/mistakes/): no CSS transitions on properties GSAP animates; prefer `fromTo()` where a `from()` may restart
- [web.dev, "How to create high-performance CSS animations"](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide): animate `transform` and `opacity`, avoid properties that trigger layout or paint
- [web.dev, "Animations and performance"](https://web.dev/articles/animations-and-performance): CSS animations of transform and opacity can run on the compositor thread, off the main thread
- [W3C, Understanding SC 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide): moving content that starts on its own, lasts over five seconds and sits beside other content needs a pause; one control for all is preferred
- [DebugBear, "Opacity animations and poor LCP"](https://www.debugbear.com/blog/opacity-animation-poor-lcp) and [Shopify, "Don't hide the LCP image behind animations"](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/best-practices/performance/dont-hide-lcp-image-behind-animations): Chrome does not count an element at opacity 0 for LCP, so fading the largest element in delays LCP
- [W3C, Understanding SC 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html): non-essential motion triggered by interaction, including scroll effects such as parallax, must be possible to turn off; `prefers-reduced-motion` techniques C39 and SCR40

## Homepage concept 2

Searched on 26 October 2026. The results were mostly agency blog posts and portfolio pages, so they informed the direction rather than proved it; the homepages of the large executive search firms were not reviewed directly.

- [Big Human, "B2B website design", 19 May 2026](https://www.bighuman.com/blog/b2b-website-design): B2B sites serve buying committees that decide together, so structure by audience and depth over impulse tactics
- [K6 Agency, "B2B Website Design in 2026: What High-Performing Sites Do Differently"](https://www.k6agency.com/b2b-website-design/): short, scannable proof blocks over long narrative; load speed and stable rendering as trust signals
- [UXPin, "Corporate Website Design: 10 Inspiring Examples & Best Practices (2026)"](https://uxpin.com/studio/blog/corporate-website-design-examples): clean typography and generous whitespace as the source of a premium feel (one example, not a guideline)
- [Living Group, Odgers Berndtson case study](https://www.living-group.com/our-work/case-studies/odgers-berndtson/): an executive search firm whose redesign moved its brand story out from under weak visuals to bold, image-led pages
- [NN/g, "Auto-Forwarding Carousels and Accordions Annoy Users and Reduce Visibility", Jakob Nielsen, 19 January 2013](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/auto-forwarding/): the reason concept 2 shows all three hero messages at once and nothing moves on its own
