# Project brief

What TRC asked for, in plain terms. Taken from two client files received on 23 October 2026: `TRC_Tech_Hiring_Developer_Brief.docx` and `Website Ideas for TRC.xlsx`.

## Who the client is

TRC Corporate Consulting Pvt. Ltd. is an existing consulting company in India. Its own site is trcconsulting.org.

This website is not for the whole company. It is for a **new division**: a technology and product recruitment practice that will carry the TRC name.

## What the new division does

It is a recruitment agency for tech roles. A product or IT company needs a backend engineer, a product manager or a CTO. Instead of searching itself, it asks TRC. TRC finds suitable people and sends their profiles. The hiring company is the paying client, not the candidate.

- **Permanent roles only.** No temporary or contract staffing.
- **Mid-level and above.** Experienced engineers at the entry point, CTOs and CPOs at the top.

## What the website must do

The brief names three jobs:

1. **Tell companies what TRC does and who it hires for.** The site sells the service.
2. **Let candidates apply directly.** A candidate fills in a form and uploads a resume.
3. **Let product and IT companies reach TRC for their hiring.** An enquiry form.

### Why candidates apply directly

The brief's vision is a ready pool of 5,000+ candidates from top-tier colleges and product companies. When a client asks for a senior data engineer, TRC wants to open its own database and send good profiles the same day, rather than start searching from zero.

So the site is also the tool that fills that database. Every candidate who applies makes TRC faster for the next client.

## What this site is not

It is **not a job portal** like Bayt.com or Naukri.

| | Job portal (Bayt) | Recruitment agency (TRC) |
|---|---|---|
| Who does the matching | The candidate searches and applies | TRC's recruiters match people to roles |
| Job listings | Yes, tens of thousands | None |
| Candidate accounts and logins | Yes | No |
| Employer dashboards | Yes | No |
| Who sees the candidate database | Paying employers search it | Only TRC's own recruiters |
| Size of build | A software product with a permanent engineering team | Around 13 pages, two forms and a place to store submissions |

On Bayt the candidate does the work. On TRC's site, TRC does the work: the candidate leaves their details and waits for a call.

If TRC ever wants a portal, the precedent is Careernet, which keeps its agency site separate and runs a candidate product on a different domain (mycareernet.co). That would be a separate project.
