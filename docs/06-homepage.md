# Homepage plan

The homepage, top to bottom, with the research behind each choice. Agreed as the plan on 23 October 2026 and built the same day; it runs at `/` in the Next.js project. Notes on how it was built are at the end.

## What the research says

| Finding | Source | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| 57% of viewing time is above the fold; 74% in the first two screens | NN/g, April 2018 | Positioning, both doors and proof go in the first two screens |
| Auto-rotating carousels hide content and get ignored like ads | NN/g, January 2013 | No carousel |
| Say who you are at a glance, show examples early, limit motion, no popups unless the law requires one | NN/g, March 2024 | Text-led hero, no audience popup |
| Hidden navigation cuts discoverability by more than 20% | NN/g, June 2016 | Visible desktop nav; on mobile the main call to action stays outside the menu |
| Short forms, one column, labels above | NN/g, May 2016 | Forms on their own pages, not on the homepage |
| India web traffic is 77.3% mobile | StatCounter, September 2026 | Mobile first. This is all Indian traffic, not TRC's audience; check against real analytics after launch |
| Good Core Web Vitals: LCP 2.5s or less, INP 200ms or less, CLS 0.1 or less, at the 75th percentile | web.dev | No hero video, static rendering, minimal JavaScript |
| WCAG 2.2: a sticky header must not fully hide a focused item (2.4.11); targets at least 24px (2.5.8) | W3C | Scroll padding under the header; 44px buttons |
| FAQ rich results limited to well-known government and health sites | Google, 8 August 2023 | The FAQ is for people, not for search features |
| DPDP Rules notified November 2025; main obligations from 13 May 2027 | PIB, DSCI | The homepage collects no personal data; privacy links in the footer |

## Section by section

### 1. Header

- **Desktop:** logo (links home), What we do (dropdown with the four pillars), Why us, Team, Contact. Right side: "Apply as a candidate" (outline) and "Hire tech talent" (solid green).
- **Mobile:** logo, a compact "Hire talent" button, a Menu button. The drawer holds Home, all links and both calls to action at full width.
- **Sticky**, turning compact with `shadow-md` after scroll. `scroll-padding-top` equals the header height so nothing focused or linked hides behind it.
- **Home link:** the logo on every page, plus the word "Home" in the mobile drawer and the footer (NN/g principle 1).

### 2. Hero

- **The page's only H1.** Draft options for TRC to choose from:
  - "Permanent tech and product hiring, from experienced engineers to CTOs." (Built as the default. "Experienced" replaces the earlier "senior", because the brief's scope starts at mid-level.)
  - "Hire the engineers, product leaders and AI specialists your roadmap needs."
  - "Technology and product talent for product and IT companies."
- **Sub-copy** from the brief: permanent hiring across technology, product, data and AI, and engineering leadership, for product and IT companies.
- **Calls to action:** "Hire tech talent" (primary), "Apply as a candidate" (secondary).
- **Three slides,** full screen: For companies (the H1, both doors), For candidates ("Your next role, in tech, product or data.") and Since 1999 ("Specialist hiring, backed by a firm founded in 1999.", with 1999 and 400+ as published at trcconsulting.org). Only the first slide's heading is an H1; the others are H2s.
- **Visual:** a deck of three slide cards beside the headline, over a photo per slide (see As built). The review build uses stock photos, preview only, until TRC supplies its own. No video.

### 3. Proof strip

- Three or four StatTiles. A tile renders only with a verified figure and a source.
- With fewer than three verified figures, show the parent's published credentials, labelled as the parent's: "TRC Corporate Consulting: 27+ years, 400+ clients."
- Leave "5,000+" out until TRC confirms it is a separate count from the parent's "5,000+ projects".

### 4. What we do

Four PillarCards, each linking to its pillar page and listing all of its roles. This puts the full role list on the homepage without a separate section, and it is the main internal-linking point.

### 5. Who we hire for

Six compact tiles: Product & SaaS, FinTech/BFSI technology, Start-ups and scale-ups, IT/ITES, Mid-market, Digital transformation programmes. One line each.

- **As built,** redesigned twice at the agency's request; the second version replaced a bento grid of cards. On desktop it is a **hiring map**: the TRC mark sits in a teal circle at the centre, with a soft green halo, and the six kinds of organisation sit around it on a ring, clockwise from the top, each joined to the centre by a dashed spoke. Each stop is a round button with an animated icon and a short label (Product & SaaS, FinTech / BFSI, Start-ups, Mid-market, IT & ITeS, Transformation), placed on the far side of the stop from the centre so no spoke crosses a label.
- **Selecting a stop** (click, or the arrow keys, Home and End; the stops follow the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern) turns it green, makes its spoke solid green with dashes running outwards from TRC, sends a ring out from the centre, and shows that organisation in the teal panel under the heading: its icon, "03 of 06", the full name and the description, with previous and next buttons.
- **Autoplay,** at the agency's request: the map tours the six stops on its own, five seconds each, with a green progress bar across the top of the panel. It follows the WAI-ARIA carousel pattern: the tour pauses while the pointer or keyboard focus is on the map or panel and while the map is off screen, ends once someone picks a stop or uses the arrows, and has a round pause and play button beside the arrows, which WCAG 2.2.2 requires for movement that starts on its own and runs longer than five seconds. It is off by default when the device asks for reduced motion. While touring, the panel does not announce each change to screen readers; once paused, it does.
- **Animated icons,** drawn for this section on the site's icon grid: chart bars rise in the product window, the bank's roof settles and columns rise, the rocket hovers with a flickering flame, the office windows light up in turn, the server lights blink and its data line runs, the transform arrows turn round a pulsing core. Every icon plays once as the map scrolls into view; the selected one plays three times; any stop loops while the pointer is over it. Nothing animates for more than about five seconds on its own, and nothing moves with reduced motion set.
- **Phones and tablets:** the same six stops on a vertical dashed line, with every name and description showing; each icon plays as its stop scrolls into view. The section is 1,188px tall on a 375px phone.
- The map's shapes carry no figures, so nothing in it can be read as a claim.

### 6. How it works

- Two columns of four numbered steps. Stacked on mobile, companies first. The numbering is justified because the steps are a real sequence.
- **Companies:** brief, shortlist, interviews, offer and joining.
- **Candidates:** submit profile, a call when a role fits, interviews, offer. The last line reassures on privacy.
- **Content comes from TRC's real process.** No reply time is promised anywhere; the old "back within two working days" demo line has been removed.

### 7. Why TRC

- Three or four points from the brief and the parent site: specialist in IT, product, AI and leadership hiring; permanent roles from mid-level to CTO; the candidate pool (only once verified); backed by TRC Corporate Consulting, in consulting since 1999.
- **Team teaser:** 2 to 4 people, when names and photos arrive. Links to Why Us and Team.
- **Offices:** TRC confirms which are current and which serve tech hiring.
- **As built,** redesigned at the agency's request as **stacking cards**. On desktop the heading and a pill linking to TRC Corporate Consulting stay pinned on the left while three cards on the right slide up and stack as the page scrolls, each sticking a little lower than the one before so every top edge stays visible. Below 1024px the cards follow one another.
  - **01 Focus,** deep teal: "Technology and product roles only", with the four practices as tiles, each with an icon and its number of role specialisms (6, 5, 5 and 4, counted from the brief).
  - **02 Level,** green: "Permanent hiring, mid-level to CTO", with a seniority ladder (Mid-level, Lead, Engineering Manager, Head of Engineering, Director, CTO / CPO, all named in the brief's role lists). Its bars grow, lowest first, as the card comes into view; under reduced motion they are simply shown.
  - **03 Backing,** light: "Part of TRC Corporate Consulting", with 1999 and 400+ as large figures, both published at trcconsulting.org.
  - The candidate-pool placeholder stays preview-only, below the stack so it never covers a card. Its Draft marker was removed at the agency's request; the card itself still disappears with preview off.

### 8. Client logos (phase 2)

Renders only with 6 or more logos, each with written permission. Sits between Why TRC and Testimonials, so the parent firm's clients back up Why TRC's "consulting firm behind it" and lead into the quotes (moved from under the proof strip at the agency's request, 26 October 2026, so the two proof bands are not stacked). A slowly scrolling strip that pauses on hover; with reduced motion set it does not move and the logos wrap into centred rows.

### 9. Testimonials (phase 2)

Renders only with 2 or more approved quotes, each with name, role and company. A static grid, no carousel.

### 10. Latest insights (phase 2)

Renders only with 3 or more published posts. Three cards linking into Insights.

### 11. FAQ

- Up to six questions in native `<details>` elements, so no JavaScript.
- **Companies:** Which roles do you cover? Do you do contract hiring? (No, permanent only.) How do fees work? Which locations do you serve?
- **Candidates:** Do you charge candidates? What happens after I apply? How is my data used?
- TRC writes every answer. No fee or charge answers are drafted on TRC's behalf.

### 12. Dual-path band

The DualPathBand component. Companies door first in source order on every page.

- **As built,** rebuilt at the agency's request: a two-tone heading, "Start with a conversation, / whichever side you are on.", with "Prefer to talk first?" and the phone number on the right. Below it, two doors: deep teal for companies, brand green for candidates, with a round "or" badge cut into the seam between them.
- **Each door:** a label, a large title, one line of copy, a short list of what the form asks for (taken from the form spec, so nobody is surprised on the next page), and the call to action with a round arrow handle. The whole door is the link. The logo's bracket sits in the top corner as a watermark.
- **Motion:** on screens 1280px and wider, the door under the pointer or keyboard focus opens wider (30%) while the other narrows; the arrow turns from diagonal to straight and the bracket eases towards the corner. The copy has a fixed width, so nothing re-wraps. No movement with reduced motion set.
- **Focus:** the focus ring is drawn outside the door, so it shows against the page in both themes.

### 13. Footer

Rebuilt at the agency's request. A full-width deep teal band, edge to edge, with its content in the same 1200px column as the rest of the page (it began as an inset rounded panel; the agency asked for full width with no outline).

- **Top:** on the left the reversed logo, the line "Permanent tech and product hiring, for product and IT companies." (second half muted) and a pill linking to TRC Corporate Consulting. On the right a compact contact block, redesigned at the agency's request to take less height: email and phone as two pill-shaped links side by side (a green round icon, a small "Email us" or "Call us" label, the value, and an arrow that turns on hover), then one card split in two, the head office address on the left and the offices on the right, grouped by country with the country name in green. At 1280px wide the block is 243px tall, level with the left column; the pills stack full width on phones.
- **Links:** What we do (the four practices); Company (Home, Why us, Team, Contact); For companies (Hire tech talent, How it works, Insights); For candidates (Apply, Questions, How we use your data). On hover the logo's bracket slides in beside the link.
- **Bottom row:** © TRC Corporate Consulting Pvt. Ltd., Privacy policy, Terms, and Back to top.
- **LinkedIn** appears once TRC supplies the URL; until then nothing shows (listed in Open questions).
- **Phase 2:** role-page links under each pillar, a smaller version of Toptal's link footer.

## Optional elements

- **Cookie notice:** only if TRC's legal team says analytics needs consent. A bottom bar, never a blocking modal.
- **WhatsApp button:** needs a WhatsApp Business number and a line in the privacy notice. Decide in phase 2.

## As built

What the first build does, beyond the plan above.

- **Preview mode.** A switch in `src/content/site.ts` shows placeholder content (stock photos, the parent's client logos and contact details, dummy quotes and answers), Draft markers on content that needs facts or approval from TRC, and dashed "Reserved, phase 2" slots where client logos, testimonials and insights will go. Turning it off for launch removes all of them at once, and lets search engines index the page. The grey review bar that used to explain this at the top of every page was removed at the agency's request on 26 October 2026; the Design system and Project docs pages are still at `/design-system/` and `/docs/`.
- **Hero,** rebuilt at the agency's request as a **full-screen card deck.** Four designs were built for comparison (split stage, full-bleed wipe, role spotlight, card deck); the agency chose the card deck on 26 October 2026 and the other three were removed. The hero fills the screen below the header (100svh less the 77px header and the 1px scroll sentinel above it). The pill, the H1, one sentence and both buttons stay fixed on the left. On the right, a deck of three cards, one per slide (For companies, For candidates, Since 1999), plays on its own, seven seconds a card, with a timer bar under the deck. Drag or swipe the front card more than 80px to send it to the back, or use the arrows. The first card does not repeat the headline beside it: under "Four practices, one team." and the line "20 role specialisms, from experienced engineers to CTO and CPO.", it shows the four practices as tiles in a two by two grid, each with its icon, name and role count, linking to its practice page (filled out at the agency's request; the plain list left the card looking empty); cards behind the front one show as coloured edges only. The hero is brand teal with white text, and behind it, at the agency's request, each card has its own photo (an engineering team, a developer at a laptop, a client meeting) that fades in while that card is at the front and settles from a slight zoom over its seven seconds. The photos are greyscale at a quarter opacity on the teal, so white text keeps at least 4.5:1 over the brightest pixel; the first card's teal is a shade lighter so it stands off the background. The photos are free Unsplash stock, marked as placeholders, so they render in preview only: with preview off the hero is plain teal (tested). In preview the first photo is preloaded and may count as the largest element; with preview off the H1 is. Autoplay pauses on hover, on keyboard focus and when the hero is off screen, stops for good once someone picks a card, has a pause button (WCAG 2.2.2), and is off with reduced motion set. Left and right arrow keys change cards. Cards behind the front one are hidden from screen readers and the keyboard. At 1280 by 800 and 1024 by 768 the deck and its controls sit fully above the fold; on a 375px phone the deck sits under the buttons and the hero is 1,214px tall; the deck is 432px tall from 640px wide and 480px below, so the longest card still fits on a 360px phone.
- **Headline sizes.** 34px on phones, 44px from 640px, 56px from 1280px, so the headline never runs past four lines.
- **Two-tone headings, everywhere.** At the agency's request, every section title and the hero headline (all three drafts) use the testimonial section's style: a lead in full ink, then a tail on its own line in `ink-500` (60% white on the dark How it works band). Titles: "Four practices, / one team." · "Built for product / and IT companies." · "Two tracks, / one outcome." · "A specialist practice, / with a consulting firm behind it." · "Trusted by teams that build, / and the people who join them." · "Notes from the hiring desk, / for companies and candidates." · "What people ask us first, / and our answers." A space sits between the two parts so screen readers read a sentence, not "build,and". Every section title is the same size (34px, `display-md`); only the hero H1 is larger.
- **Proof strip.** Two groups. "TRC Corporate Consulting": 27+ years and 400+ clients, both published at trcconsulting.org. "This practice": 4 hiring practices and 20 role specialisms, both counted from the brief. No candidate-pool figure. Redesigned at the agency's request: all four figures sit on one grid (four equal columns from 1024px, two below), so every figure, label and source lines up across both groups, and each tile's three lines share rows with its neighbour so they stay level when a label wraps. Each group is labelled with the green bracket and a hairline across its two columns. The figures count up from zero once as the strip scrolls into view (1.6 seconds, easing out); the final value is what the server renders, what screen readers hear and what shows with reduced motion, and it holds the width so the "+" never shifts. The "+" takes the accent green; labels are 17px semibold.
- **What we do,** redesigned twice at the agency's request; a tile grid with animated pictures in between was replaced because the agency wanted icons and horizontal cards with a scroll interaction. Now: **landscape practice cards in a scroll-driven horizontal track.** On screens at least 1024px wide and 740px tall the section pins as it reaches the top, and the page's vertical scroll slides the row of cards sideways; a counter (01 / 04) and a green progress bar beside the heading follow along; once the last card is in view the page carries on. Each card is a white frame around a flat panel in the Latest insights palette (teal, light green, neutral, green) with the practice icon in a circle (code, layers, spark, flag; it tilts on hover), a large faint number, the practice name and summary, every role as a chip, the role count, and a round arrow. The whole card links to the practice page. The row ends with a dashed "See how each practice works" card linking to the What we do page. Keyboard: tabbing to a card scrolls the page to the point where that card is in view. Below 1024px, on shorter screens, or with reduced motion set, the row is a native swipe row instead (957px tall on a 375px phone).
- **How it works,** redesigned twice at the agency's request; a two-lane track board in between was dropped as confusing. Now: a two-column heading area, bottoms aligned: the eyebrow and heading on the left; on the right, right-aligned, the intro and, under it, a large **track switch**, both flush with the right edge of the last card, so the heading area and the cards share one frame (settled after two rounds of agency feedback: the switch first floated unaligned on the right, then left the right half empty when stacked on the left). The switch reads "For companies" or "For candidates" (with a building and a person icon, and a green pill that slides to the chosen side; WAI-ARIA tabs, arrow keys, Home and End). Under it, four cards in one row joined by round arrows: the chosen track's steps 01 to 03, each with a small illustrated scene, a step pill, a title and one line of detail (see Step cards below), then a green card 04, "Offer and joining" (its "Same for both tracks" tag and the section's Draft marker were removed at the agency's request; the steps are still listed in Open questions for TRC to confirm).
